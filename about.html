Our Story

Built for Ethiopian travellers

ET Transport was built to make intercity bus travel across Ethiopia simple, transparent and reliable — compare operators, pick your seat and book in minutes, without phone calls, queues or paper tickets.

From daily commutes to Adama to long hauls north of the capital.
