About ET Transport

Travel Ethiopia the Easy Way

Your trusted partner for intercity bus travel in Ethiopia.

Our Story

ET Transport was built to make intercity bus travel across Ethiopia simple, transparent and reliable. We bring every major bus operator together in one place, so you can compare schedules and prices, pick your favourite seat and book your journey in minutes — without phone calls, queues or paper tickets.

Our Mission

Connecting cities and communities across Ethiopia with comfortable, dependable and affordable travel. We work closely with trusted, verified operators to keep every journey safe, on time and stress-free — from Addis Ababa to Bahir Dar, Hawassa, Mekelle, Dire Dawa and beyond.
